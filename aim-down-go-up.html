About Aim Down, Go Up

Five levels of recoil-only movement through zero, weak and normal gravity on an abandoned orbital station. You have three guns and every shot pushes you the opposite way: aim down to go up, fire ahead to brake. Falling doesn't kill you. It costs you the ground you gained.
