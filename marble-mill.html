ASMR mode

One key hides the whole interface. Just the machine and the chimes.

The mill is an instrument

Every hit is pitch-quantized to a pentatonic scale, so a thousand random collisions come out as a chime melody instead of noise. Each era raises the root a fifth — you can hear your progress with your eyes closed.

At full tilt the mill fires around 1,900 collisions a second. It doesn't get louder — it gets denser.

Copper C a muted wooden tock
Silver G brighter, rounder
Gold D bell-like
Diamond A glass

Good to know

There is no offline income — the mill runs while you're watching it. This is a game you sit with, not a tab you check. Runs on integrated graphics; it's meant for a laptop on a quiet evening.
