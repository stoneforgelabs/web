An open lab for small games

StoneForge Labs is an independent game studio in Czechia. We put small playable ideas out early, listen to the people who play them, and keep building the ones that click.

How we choose: what you play counts most, then Steam wishlists and how many of you ask for more. No single number decides — but we read every signal.

No dark patterns. No microtransactions, no “wait or pay” timers, no ads.
