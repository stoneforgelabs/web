Setup

The app's home page has the same four steps and marks each one Done.

  1. Schedule. Enter how many units you can make on each weekday. Use 0 for days you're closed. Then set:
    • Minimum notice: how many days ahead customers must order. 0 allows same-day pickup.
    • Daily order cut-off: after this time, the earliest pickup day moves one day later. It uses your store's time zone.
    • Booking horizon: how far ahead customers can book.
  2. Products. Add the products you make to order and give them effort units. You can change units per variant later.
  3. Theme. Open the theme editor from the app, add the Pickup date block to your product template and save.
  4. Test order. Pick a date on a product, check out, and make sure the order shows up on that day in Calendar.

Effort units

Units tell the app how much of your day a product takes. You pick the numbers. They don't have to match hours.

Example: a mini cake is 3 units, a birthday cake 8, a wedding cake 15. With 30 units a day, one of each leaves 4 units free.

All products you add share the same daily total. Quantity counts, so two birthday cakes take 16 units.

Daily use

Home

Shows the next 14 days: units booked, units left and a status (Open, Full, Closed, Within notice, Ordering closed). Needs attention lists things to check, such as an order that skipped the online checkout.

Calendar

Open a day to see its orders and units. You can change that day's units, close it, or add a note only you can see.

Lowering or closing a day doesn't cancel orders you've already taken.

Check now

Compares each day's units with your recent orders. The app also does this once a day.

Recent changes

The Schedule page lists recent changes to your settings.

The capacity product

The app adds a product called Production capacity (DateCapacity Guard) to your store. It has one variant per pickup date, costs 0, and its stock is that day's free units. When a customer adds a cake, the matching units go into the cart with it. Checkout won't go through if the day has run out.

Don't delete, unpublish or edit this product. It has to stay on the Online Store for checkout to work. It's hidden from search engines. If you use automated collections or product feeds, leave it out of them.

Limits

Only orders placed through the Online Store checkout are checked. These are not:

  • orders created or edited by staff, including draft orders
  • POS orders
  • orders created by other apps

The app lists them under Needs attention when it finds them, but it can't block them. For phone orders, check the units left for that day first.

OrderPace works with one kitchen and one daily total. It doesn't plan deliveries, staff or several locations.

FAQ

What does the customer see?

A calendar on the product page. Days that are full, closed, too soon or past the cut-off can't be picked. Full days say Fully booked. If a day fills up while they shop, they're asked to pick another one.

Does it work with Shop Pay, Apple Pay or other express buttons?

We haven't tested these yet. The app hides the Buy it now button on your made-to-order products so customers go through the cart. Contact us before you rely on express checkout.

Can I keep my current date picker app?

We haven't tested OrderPace with other date picker apps, and the two won't share data. Contact us before using both on the same products.

Can customers pick a time?

No, only a date.

Does the app store customer data?

No. For each order it keeps the order ID and number, the pickup date and the units. No names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. See the privacy policy.

What languages does the date picker use?

Its messages are in English. Month and day names follow your storefront's language. Today and the cut-off use your store's time zone.

Turning off and uninstalling

To pause the app, go to Schedule and click Turn off. Checkout stops checking dates, the capacity product goes to draft and the date picker disappears. Your settings stay. Turn on brings everything back.

Turn the app off before you uninstall it. Once uninstalled, the app can't clean up your store. After uninstalling, remove the Pickup date block from your theme and delete the capacity product.

When you uninstall, we delete your store's data. See data retention.
