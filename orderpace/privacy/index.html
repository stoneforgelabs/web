Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

OrderPace doesn't store your customers' names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. For each order it keeps the order ID and number, the pickup date and the units. We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies. Data is stored in the Netherlands and deleted after you uninstall.

1. Who we are

OrderPace is run by StoneForge Labs, s.r.o., Nové sady 988/2, 602 00 Brno, Czechia, Company ID 24546844.

This policy covers the OrderPace Shopify app and these pages. We are the controller of data about you as a merchant. For order data from your store, we process it for you, under Shopify's API terms and this policy.

2. What we store

When you install the app, you give it access to products, inventory, sales channels, checkout validation and orders (read only). It stores:

Data the app stores
DataDetails
Store.myshopify.com address, time zone, currency, location, IDs of the product and checkout rule the app creates, install and uninstall dates, last sync status.
Access tokenThe token Shopify issues so the app can work with your store.
SettingsDaily units, notice, cut-off, booking horizon, changes to single days and your notes on them.
ProductsIDs and names of the products and variants you add, their units, and the stock set for each date.
OrdersOrder ID, order number (like #1001), pickup date, units, whether it was cancelled, when it last changed.
History and alertsChanges to settings and products, with the time and the staff member's Shopify user ID (a number, no name or email). Alerts shown in the app.
Webhook IDsIDs of messages from Shopify, so each is handled once.
LogsOur host logs requests to the app: time, URL, status, IP address and browser. The app logs which store a Shopify message was for, and errors.
Support emailYour address, your message and attachments.

Shopify sends the whole order to the app, which can include customer details. The app reads only product and variant IDs, quantities and the pickup date it added itself. It doesn't store the rest.

3. What we don't do

  • Store customer names, emails, phone numbers, addresses or payment details.
  • Sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Use analytics, ads or tracking cookies. These pages load no scripts and nothing from other sites.
  • Use your store's data to train AI models.

4. The date picker

The Pickup date block runs in your customers' browsers. It reads the dates and free units the app saves in your store, reads the cart, and adds the product with the chosen date. It sends nothing to us and stores nothing on the customer's device. The date is saved in the order as a line item property, inside your Shopify store.

5. Why

  • To run the app: keep each day's units in sync with your settings, check orders at checkout, show bookings and flag problems. Legal basis: contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep it secure and working: logs, fixing errors, change history. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To answer support emails. Legal basis: contract or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b), (f)).
  • To meet legal duties, such as accounting (Art. 6(1)(c)).

6. Service providers

  • Google Cloud hosts the app and its database in the Netherlands (europe-west4).
  • Shopify runs the platform. Your store's data is covered by Shopify's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts these pages and logs visitors' IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Our email providers handle support emails.

App data stays in the EU. If a provider handles data outside the EEA, it does so under GDPR safeguards such as the EU standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

  • While the app is installed, we keep your data so it can work.
  • When you uninstall, the access token is deleted right away.
  • 48 hours later Shopify asks us to erase your store's data, and the app deletes all of it.
  • Logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • Database backups expire on their own. Deleted data is gone once they do.
  • Support emails are kept while we need them to help you.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The app checks that every message really comes from Shopify. Each record belongs to one store, and the app only works with the store that's signed in. Secrets are kept in Google Cloud Secret Manager, not in code. Only the people who run the app can access production.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, limit how we use it, or object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email us and we'll reply within a month. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, ÚOOÚ, or the authority where you live.

10. Requests from your customers

When a customer asks Shopify about their data, Shopify passes the request to the app. The app has no customer details, so there's usually nothing to send. When Shopify asks it to erase a customer's data, it deletes the bookings for that customer's orders. Customers can also contact your store directly.

11. Changes

If the app starts storing new kinds of data, we'll update this page first and change the date at the top.
