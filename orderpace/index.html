Match cake orders to your kitchen's daily capacity

OrderPace is a Shopify app for made-to-order shops. Customers pick a pickup date on the product page, and only days with room for their order are open.

How it works

  • Give each product effort units. A wedding cake takes more of your day than a box of cupcakes.
  • Set how many units you can make each weekday. Close days, and set notice and a daily cut-off.
  • Customers pick a date that still has room. Checkout checks it again.
  • See booked and free units for every day in a calendar.

Limits

One kitchen, one daily total. POS orders, draft orders, order edits and orders from other apps aren't checked; the app flags them for you. More about limits
